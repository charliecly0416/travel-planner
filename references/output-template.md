# Itinerary Output Template

Adapt this template to the requested language and depth. Do not force empty sections on a day when they do not apply.
Use the traveler's meal count. Do not add a breakfast or snack stop unless requested; show meals as distinct entries between the relevant time blocks.

## Trip overview

- Dates and cities, with local time zones and overnight/date-line changes.
- Booked anchors: flights, lodging, purchased tickets, intercity transport.
- Traveler priorities, walking intensity, meal pattern, and daily budget.
- Key assumptions and information checked date.

## Daily schedule

### YYYY-MM-DD — City / transfer

**Local base:** lodging name and searchable address. Include where luggage stays.

**早上（午餐前）**

- `08:00–09:00` Lodging → Searchable Stop A. Walk or transit details; bag status. Do not insert a meal unless it is part of the traveler's stated meal plan.
- `09:00–11:30` Stop A → Stop B → Stop C. Use arrows only when the chain is on foot. State what to see and whether this is a quick photo stop or a longer visit.

**午餐｜11:30–12:15**

- Restaurant branch — order — local price and estimated home-currency amount; say whether tax/tip are included.

**下午（晚餐前）**

- `12:15–13:00` Stop C to the next cluster. If transport is required, name the mode/line/direction/transfer in a separate row.
- `13:00–16:30` Searchable stops in walking order; note admission, reservation, and useful visit length.

**晚餐｜17:00–18:00**

- Restaurant branch — order — budget — reservation/queue and dietary notes.

**晚上**

- Include evening walk, ferry, viewpoint, return to lodging, or next transport. State how to return and when to collect bags.

**如延误/天气差：** Preserve booked transport and the highest-priority experience; name the first optional stop to drop. Give a concrete fallback.

**交通预算：** Local currency and home-currency range, with major legs and exclusions.

## Booking checklist

| Date | Item | Status | Where/how to buy | Price basis | Deadline / caveat |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| YYYY-MM-DD | Attraction / major transport | 已购 / 建议预订 / 可现场买 / 待核 / 备选 | Official or requested channel | Current price or estimate | Entry slot, schedule release, refund terms |

## Budget summary

Summarize meals by day and transport by city/leg. Separate admission, optional activities, shopping, and local transit so a package is not mistaken for an all-inclusive price. State exchange-rate assumptions and whether tips/taxes are included.

## Practical notes

Include only trip-relevant notes: lodging/bag handling, airport check-in and gate timing, payment/cash, weather/daylight, safety/accessibility, and current uncertainty.
