# Planning Checklist

Use this checklist during construction and final review. A checked box means the fact is resolved or clearly labeled as uncertain, not that every item applies to every trip.

## Inputs and hard constraints

- [ ] Correct year, dates, weekdays, local times, time zones, and date-line changes.
- [ ] City order, arrival/departure endpoints, and booked flight/train/ferry times.
- [ ] Lodging exact address and booking nights.
- [ ] Check-in, checkout, early bag-drop, and luggage collection are feasible.
- [ ] Baggage size/weight and airline fare rules are accounted for.
- [ ] Passport/entry requirements are not inferred beyond available evidence.
- [ ] Pace, walking, accessibility, nature/city balance, photos, shopping, meals, and budget are explicit.

## Route quality

- [ ] Attractions are clustered by area and connected from lodging.
- [ ] Scenic or landmark detours are judged by value, not distance alone.
- [ ] Redundant/low-value stops and unrequested shopping filler are removed.
- [ ] Hotels, airports, stations, and piers appear wherever the traveler must act there.
- [ ] Every luggage transfer is explicit; no bag is carried across an attraction by accident.
- [ ] Arrows only connect consecutive walkable stops.
- [ ] Transit rows name route/operator, direction, transfer, and destination.
- [ ] Walking duration includes realistic access and conditions.
- [ ] Photo stops have short durations; nature/waterfront time is not reduced to a photo-only visit when the traveler wants to linger.
- [ ] Fixed tickets and transport have arrival/check-in buffers.
- [ ] A weather, delay, closure, and fatigue fallback identifies what to drop first.

## Research and bookings

- [ ] Exact-day opening hours, last entry, holiday hours, and seasonal operation checked.
- [ ] Timetables for the requested year checked, or explicitly provisional.
- [ ] Correct terminal, station, entrance, and pier identified.
- [ ] Fare, airport surcharge, fare cap, card acceptance, and booking requirement checked.
- [ ] Attraction dates, ticket terms, price, sales channel, and refund conditions checked.
- [ ] “Booked” is used only for a purchase/booking confirmed by the traveler or booking record.
- [ ] Aggregator package operator, departure point, duration, inclusions, and date are verified.
- [ ] Source link and check date retained for volatile facts.

## Meals and money

- [ ] Meal count matches the traveler's routine; flight meals are handled correctly.
- [ ] Restaurant is on route, open at arrival, and serves the proposed item.
- [ ] Backup exists for a closure, queue, or sold-out item.
- [ ] Menu price, tax, service fee, card fee, and planned tip are included or stated separately.
- [ ] Daily/meal budget totals are arithmetically correct.
- [ ] Dietary restrictions and alcohol preference are handled without unsupported guarantees.
- [ ] Cash/ATM/exchange guidance accounts for fees, operating hours, and realistic small denominations.
- [ ] Exchange rates are labeled as planning assumptions, not guaranteed quotes.

## Airport and major transport

- [ ] Lodging-to-airport route and realistic travel time shown.
- [ ] Airline counter opening and cutoff checked for that route and airport where available.
- [ ] Document check, security, immigration/preclearance, terminal transfer, and gate walk considered.
- [ ] Arrival-at-airport time is distinct from gate-arrival target and boarding/departure time.
- [ ] No generic airport buffer substitutes for route-specific reasoning.
- [ ] No-checked-bag status does not erase counter/document-check requirements.
- [ ] Rail/ferry check-in, platform/pier, advance booking, return time, and missed-departure consequences shown.
- [ ] Big-transport cost included separately from frequent local transit.

## Document quality

- [ ] Place names are searchable; addresses included when ambiguous.
- [ ] Time ranges include movement plus visit, not just stationary attraction time.
- [ ] Optional stops, backups, and priorities are visually distinct.
- [ ] No contradictory time, address, booking status, or route wording across pages.
- [ ] PDF links work; page breaks, clipped text, dense pages, and page count checked after rendering.
- [ ] Only the requested final deliverable is presented as current.
